Mendarythe refund desk that reads your policy
PayPal sandbox + Workers AI

Customers ask for refunds in their own words. Mendary checks the real PayPal order, applies your policy, and sends the refund after you tap Approve.

The AI reads and writes words. Plain code sets the money and can never go above your policy or what was paid. Nothing moves until the owner approves.

  1. 1Buy with PayPal
  2. 2Ask for a refund in plain words
  3. 3AI reads it, code sets the amount
  4. 4Owner approves, PayPal refunds
Try it yourself (sandbox, no real money). Pay with the test buyer __BUYER_EMAIL__ password __BUYER_PASS__. Then ask for a refund in column 2 and approve it in column 3. In this demo anyone can open the owner desk.
